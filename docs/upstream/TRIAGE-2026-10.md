## Done in this pass

- **Desktop: local backends are never idle-reaped** (upstream `167e9fdb`, #134942). Our reaper
  stopped any pool entry idle longer than the idle limit, including a local `hermes serve` child, which
  runs the profile's scheduled jobs and bot chats with nobody watching. Now only process-less connection
  descriptors are reclaimed (`selectIdleReclaims` in `electron/pool-eviction.ts`, with a test); the slot
  cap and foreground reclaim are unchanged. The setting's description (en, pl) says it applies to remote
  connections. Upstream's extra work (removing a pinned-tier TTL and `retireIdle`) does not apply: we never
  had those.
